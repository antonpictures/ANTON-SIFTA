## What the video claims (Marcus / Greene)

1. Scaling is plateauing; recent progress comes from the **harness** around LLMs —
   loops, conditionals, interpreters, theorem provers — which Marcus calls
   "symbolic AI / neurosymbolic". Pure LLMs fail at abstraction, out-of-distribution
   generalization, and even legal chess moves; they memorize within a cloud of
   training points and break outside it (his 1998 identity-function / odd-number
   result, still true today).
2. LLMs are statistical approximators that produce "not bad guesses"; they
   **do not attach confidence measures to claims**, which is why hallucination
   persists (Harry Shearer "no Henrietta, but…"; Greene's Renée Fleming spouse error).
3. Benchmarks are unreliable instruments (psychometrics: reliability ≠ validity);
   labs train toward them; "92% honest" still means an 8% error rate — people
   notice the numerator and not the denominator.
4. System 1 / System 2 (Kahneman) is an oversimplification but useful: nets = fast
   pattern layer, symbols = abstraction/rules layer; the field's bias has been
   empiricist "learn everything from data", while Spelke-style **core cognition**
   (object permanence, agents, places) should be built in as principled nativism,
   with learning layered on the core.
5. Evolution needs a subroutine library to leverage; evolving at the neuron level
   goes nowhere — evolve at the algorithmic/high-level subroutine level.
6. World models: LLMs do not induce the rules of a new environment (chess, Harry
   Potter, GTA); nobody currently knows how to make a system build a world model
   from observation. No consciousness meter exists; boundary cases (dog → amoeba)
   are undrawn. Long-run replacement of jobs; meaning shifts from work to art.
