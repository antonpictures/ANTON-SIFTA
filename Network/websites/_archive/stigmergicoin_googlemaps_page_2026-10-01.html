Project announcement

googlemapscoin.com was not renewed — an oversight, not an abandonment of the coin. The token itself is unaffected: it graduated, trades on DEX, and the on-chain supply is fixed. A domain expiring does not change a Solana token.

There is no second coin and there will not be one — the original already holds the supply and the liquidity. Creating a duplicate would only split the community.
