Everything this site must tell you, in one place.

This is not financial advice

Nothing on this site is financial advice, a recommendation, or a solicitation to buy anything. The desk answers questions; it does not manage money, take custody of anything, or act on your behalf. Every decision, and every consequence, is yours.

Meme coins are extremely high risk

  • Most meme coins lose most or all of their value. Independent tracking of this platform has put the collapse rate above 98% of tokens launched.
  • Thin liquidity means small orders move the price sharply, and you may not be able to sell at the price shown.
  • Bundled launches, wash trading and coordinated dumps are common. A price chart can be manufactured.
  • Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose entirely.

Where the numbers come from

Prices and market figures come from public data sources (GeckoTerminal, DexScreener, Yahoo Finance) and news headlines from public RSS feeds (BBC, CNBC, CoinDesk, MarketWatch). They can be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. When a source is down this site says so rather than inventing a figure. Verify anything you intend to act on against a source of your own.

What is recorded about visitors

This site remembers its visitors so the host can see who returns and what people ask about.

  • A first-party cookie (sifta_vid) identifies your browser. It holds no personal details, only a random identifier.
  • Each visit is recorded with the page, the time, your browser's own user-agent string, the referring page, and your language setting.
  • IP addresses are stored only as a salted hash, never as a raw address. The hash cannot be turned back into an address without the local secret.
  • Questions you ask the desk are stored with the answer, so Alice can remember the conversation. Do not send personal, financial or confidential information through the chat box.
  • Records are kept for 180 days and then pruned automatically.
  • You can be forgotten. Ask the host, or write to the address below, and the record plus its file are deleted.

There is no advertising, no third-party analytics, no tracking pixel and no device fingerprinting on this site. A different browser reading as a different visitor is the honest cost of that choice.
